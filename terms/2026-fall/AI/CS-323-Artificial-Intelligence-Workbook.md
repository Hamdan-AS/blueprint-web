## Introduction

The Laboratory Workbook supports the Practical Sessions of the course Artificial Intelligence (CS-323). The Workbook has been designed to cover the major areas of Artificial Intelligence including **Expert Systems**, **Machine Learning**, and **Fuzzy Logic Systems**.

**Course Learning Outcome (CLO):**
> *"Demonstrate the use of modern tools and techniques for developing intelligent systems. (C3, PLO-3)"*

All lab sessions assist the achievement of the above CLO. A rubric to evaluate student performance is provided at the end of the workbook.

- **Lab 1–5:** Machine Learning algorithms using Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) — logic functions, creating/working with ANNs, problem-solving phases, effect of external factors on ANN performance.
- **Lab 6–7:** Graph solving and searching (uninformed & informed) using Graph Solving tools.
- **Lab 8:** Consistency-based CSP solver.
- **Lab 9:** Stochastic local search-based CSP solver.
- **Lab 10–11:** Basic and advanced concepts of Expert Systems.
- **Lab 12:** Data-driven programming in Expert Systems.
- **Lab 13:** Fuzzy Logic applications using MATLAB Fuzzy Logic Toolbox and Fuzzy Tech.
- **Lab 14:** Complex engineering activity (as directed by lab instructor).
