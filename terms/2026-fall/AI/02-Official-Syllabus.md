# CS-323 — Official Syllabus

- **Credit Hours:** 3+1 (Theory 3, Practical 1)
- **Batch:** 2018 and Onwards
- **Semester:** Third Year, Fall Semester
- **Source:** Detailed Syllabi BE(CS) batch 2018 onwards (PDF)
- **Lab Source:** Practical Workbook CS-323 (NED UET, Dept. of Computer & Information Systems Engineering, revised Aug 2024 by Ms. Anita Ali, Dr. Saad Qasim Khan, Ms. Ibshar Ishrat, Ms. Tahreem Khan)

## Official Topic List

> Introduction to AI, Computational Intelligence, Searching Methodologies, First-Order-Logic, Evolutionary Computing: Genetic Algorithms, Evolutionary Strategies, Biology-Inspired Models; Knowledge Representation: Semantic Networks, Frames and Scripts; Reasoning with Imperfect Knowledge, Rule-Based Systems: Modeling, Reasoning Strategies, Conflict Resolution and Rule Matching; Artificial Neural Networks; Vague Notions in Knowledge-Based Systems: Models based on Fuzzy Set Theory; Game Theory, Prospects of Artificial Intelligence.

## Course Learning Outcome (CLO)

> "Demonstrate the use of modern tools and techniques for developing intelligent systems. (C3, PLO-3)"

## Theory Modules

### Module 1: Introduction & Computational Intelligence
- What is AI? Acting/thinking humanly vs rationally
- Computational Intelligence overview (evolutionary, fuzzy, neural paradigms)

### Module 2: Searching Methodologies
- Uninformed search: BFS, DFS, UCS, depth-limited, iterative deepening
- Informed search: greedy best-first, A*, admissible/consistent heuristics

### Module 3: First-Order Logic
- FOL syntax and semantics; unification; resolution and refutation

### Module 4: Evolutionary Computing
- Genetic Algorithms: selection, crossover, mutation
- Evolutionary Strategies: (μ, λ) vs (μ + λ), Rechenberg's 1/5 rule
- Biology-Inspired Models: PSO, ACO

### Module 5: Knowledge Representation
- Semantic Networks, Frames, Scripts

### Module 6: Reasoning with Imperfect Knowledge & Rule-Based Systems
- Reasoning with imperfect knowledge (certainty factors, Dempster-Shafer)
- Rule-Based Systems: modeling, reasoning strategies, conflict resolution, rule matching (Rete)

### Module 7: Artificial Neural Networks
- Perceptron, ADALINE, multi-layer networks, backpropagation

### Module 8: Vague Notions — Fuzzy Set Theory
- Fuzzy sets, membership functions, Mamdani/Sugeno inference, defuzzification

### Module 9: Game Theory & Prospects of AI
- Game theory, Nash equilibrium, Prisoner's dilemma
- Prospects of Artificial Intelligence (AGI, ethics, bias)
