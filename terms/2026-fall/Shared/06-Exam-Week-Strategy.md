# EXAM WEEK STRATEGY
---
## Before Each Exam
---
### Night Before

- No new material after 18:00
- Review 1-page cheat sheet (only)
- Sleep 8.5+ hours (plan for 9 to get 8)
- Prepare: pens, calculator, water, ID
---
### Morning Of

- Moderate-protein + complex-carb breakfast (eggs + oats)
- Caffeine 45-60 min before exam (if used)
- Arrive 15 min early
- Restroom before entering
---
### During Exam

1. **Scan** — 2 min, identify all questions
2. **Execute numericals first** — highest ROI, objective marks
3. **Draw diagrams second** — use pencil, label everything
4. **Write theory last** — 5-point structure for every answer
---
### Answer Structure (Theory Questions)

```
Definition      →  "X is..."
Explanation     →  "It works by..."
Example         →  "For instance..."
Advantage       →  "The benefit is..."
Disadvantage    →  "The limitation is..."
```
---
### If Stuck

- Flag the question, move on
- Return if time permits
- Partial answer > blank answer (structure gets 60% marks)
---
## Back-to-Back Exam Protocol

| Scenario | Strategy |
|----------|----------|
| Arch (Day 1) + Networks (Day 2) | Study Arch first (3 days before), Networks second (2 days before). Night between: SLEEP 9h, do NOT study |
| Stats + SE same day | Stats (analytical) in morning, SE (memorization) in afternoon. Switch costs cognitive energy — practice switching |
| AI before Networks lab | Finish AI study by evening. Submit lab as-is (partial better than zero) |
---
## Exam Sequence Protection

```
Priority Order:
  1. Sleep (non-negotiable)
  2. Hydration (2L/day)
  3. Exercise (20 min minimum)
  4. Meals (low glycemic, regular timing)
  5. Review (only active retrieval, no re-reading)
```
---
## Post-Exam Recovery

- 30 min break (walk, hydrate, snack)
- Do NOT discuss answers with classmates (anxiety spillover)
- Shift focus immediately to next exam
