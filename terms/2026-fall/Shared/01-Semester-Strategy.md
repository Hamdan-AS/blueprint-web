## Goal
Not "study everything." Maximize expected marks per hour across the entire semester.

**Total Load:** 18 credit hours

## Strategy Principles

1. **Numericals first** — objective marking, high ROI per hour
2. **Diagrams second** — procedural memory, once learned, never forgotten
3. **Theory third** — memorization-heavy, diminishing returns
4. **Group project managed** — highest variance, needs protocol not heroics

## Non-Negotiables

- 8 hours sleep every night (22:30-07:00)
- No screens after 22:00
- Exercise daily (~40 min, when free — before 08:00 or after 17:00)
- Phone in another room during study blocks
- Blank-page retrieval, never re-reading
