# Master Formula Book

## MT-335 — Probability & Statistics

### Sampling Distribution of Mean
- μ_x̄ = μ
- σ_x̄ = σ / √n

### Confidence Intervals
- CI for μ (σ known): x̄ ± z_α/2 × σ/√n
- CI for μ (σ unknown): x̄ ± t_α/2 × s/√n
- CI for proportion: p̂ ± z_α/2 × √(p̂(1−p̂)/n)
- Sample Size: n = (z_α/2 × σ / E)²

### Hypothesis Testing
- z = (x̄ − μ) / (σ/√n)   (σ known)
- t = (x̄ − μ) / (s/√n)   (σ unknown)
- Reject H₀ if |test statistic| > critical value
- Reject H₀ if p-value < α

### Chi-Square
- χ² = Σ (O − E)² / E
- Expected frequency = (row total × column total) / grand total

### Regression
- b = Σ((x − x̄)(y − ȳ)) / Σ((x − x̄)²)
- a = ȳ − b × x̄
- r = Σ((x − x̄)(y − ȳ)) / √(Σ(x − x̄)² × Σ(y − ȳ)²)
- R² = coefficient of determination (proportion of variance explained)
